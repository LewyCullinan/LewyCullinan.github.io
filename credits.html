Project notes

SmartWater, the line follower and LEO rover are personal project topics. The drone inspection is an engineering design study. Technical references are linked in the relevant project sections and downloadable briefs. No unverified performance results are claimed. The CV page and current PDF were prepared from the supplied Word CV and later details supplied by Lewis; the original Word file remains outside the public site.

Interactive demonstrations

The SmartWater example illustrates a possible irrigation controller with hysteresis. The line-follower example illustrates proportional steering. Both use simulated inputs and simplified rules; neither is a recording of a real system.
