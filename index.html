About me

I hold a First-Class Honours BEng in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from Liverpool John Moores University and am currently studying an MSc in Robotics.

My interests centre on AI-driven robotics: systems that learn, adapt and generalise in unstructured environments. I’m particularly interested in embodied intelligence, where perception, decision-making and control are learned jointly.

I want to explore reinforcement learning for control, foundation-model-based robotics and sim-to-real transfer—developing learned policies that can move from simulation into real-world systems.

CAREER INTERESTS

Robotics for harsh environments

05

I want to work on autonomous systems for complex and extreme environments, including space, nuclear infrastructure, offshore subsea systems and disaster response. I’m drawn to applications where robots can inspect, explore or carry out tasks in places that are dangerous or difficult for people to reach.

My focus is on robust AI that can handle uncertainty, partial observability and high-risk decisions. In these settings, a robot may have incomplete sensor information, unfamiliar terrain or limited contact with its operator. I’m interested in how learned perception and control can support useful behaviour while recognising uncertainty and knowing when to stop or ask for help.

I want to investigate how reinforcement learning, foundation models and sim-to-real transfer can contribute to that reliability. The challenge that interests me is getting a learned policy to adapt to changing conditions without relying on the environment behaving exactly as it did in training. That also means thinking about fault detection, fallback behaviour and the balance between autonomy and human supervision.

Longer term, I’d like a role that combines AI and embedded systems development with practical robotics testing. I want to help take robots from simulation and laboratory prototypes into representative conditions, understand where they fail and improve their reliability before deployment.
